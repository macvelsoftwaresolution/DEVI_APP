Delete your DEVI account

DEVI app by Macvel Software Solutions. You can also do this in the app: Settings → Delete account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number and address
  • Your guardians’ names and numbers
  • Your SOS history: alert times, locations and addresses, and control room notes

What is not deleted

  • WhatsApp messages already delivered to your guardians stay on their phones.
  • Records needed for an ongoing police case or legal request are kept only as long as the law requires.
  • Short-lived technical records without your name (sign-in code records, delivery statuses) are removed automatically within 30 days.

Deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. While an SOS is still being sent, wait until you have marked yourself safe.

Include the country code. A 10-digit number is read as India (+91). We send a 6-digit code on WhatsApp to prove it is your number.

No access to that WhatsApp number? Write to us using the contact in our privacy policy.

In an emergency, call 112 (Police) or 1091 (Women Helpline).
