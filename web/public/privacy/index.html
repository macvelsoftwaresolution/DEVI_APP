Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to the DEVI Android app and the DEVI control room.

DEVI is a personal safety app made by Macvel Software Solutions (“we”, “us”). When you press SOS, DEVI alerts the people you trust with your location at that moment and records short evidence. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it and how you can control it.

In short
  • Your location is collected only while an SOS is active.
  • During an SOS, the app records up to 2 minutes of video and audio as evidence.
  • Your alert goes to your guardians, and to the DEVI control room when you have no guardians.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.

Some information stays only on your phone: your first-run setup, guest guardians, the date you accepted the terms and any evidence that has not uploaded yet.

DEVI places phone calls to your guardian or emergency number through your phone’s SIM. We do not record or receive those calls.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location: read once, when you press SOS, to send where you are. DEVI does not track your location in the background and does not use it outside an SOS.
  • Camera and microphone: to record SOS evidence.
  • Phone calls: to call your guardian directly when you press SOS.
  • Contacts (optional): to let you pick a guardian from your phone’s contact list. Your contact list is read only on your phone; only the guardian you choose is saved and sent to our server. You can type the number instead and deny this permission.
  • Notifications: to keep evidence recording running when the app is in the background. During an SOS the notification reads “Devi Safety is active”, without the word SOS, so a person near you is less likely to notice an alert.

2. Who can see your information

  • Your guardians. They receive a WhatsApp message with your name, the alert time and a Google Maps link to the place where you pressed SOS. They do not get live location updates or your evidence.
  • DEVI control room staff. Authorised operators can see active and past alerts, your profile details, location and evidence, so they can coordinate help.
  • Police or emergency services, when you or your guardians ask for help, or when the law requires us to share information.
  • Service providers that run parts of DEVI for us. They process data only to provide their service:
    • Supabase: database for accounts, guardians and SOS records
    • Cloudinary: private storage for evidence video and audio
    • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers SOS messages to guardians
    • Google Maps Platform and BigDataCloud: turn coordinates into a readable address
    • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): turns coordinates into a readable address

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app, the website and our server uses HTTPS.
  • Names, addresses, guardian details, alert addresses and operator notes are encrypted in our database (AES-256-GCM).
  • Evidence files are stored privately. They open only through a protected link that needs the alert’s private key or a control room login.
  • Only the phone that started an SOS can change or close it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account, guardians, SOS history and evidence: kept until you ask us to delete them, or longer only if the law requires it (for example, an ongoing police case).
  • Data on your phone: removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Delete your DEVI account or data

This section explains how to delete your account and data in the DEVI app by Macvel Software Solutions.

How to request deletion

  1. Email CONTACT EMAIL with the subject “Delete my DEVI account”.
  2. Include your registered mobile number. If you used Instant SOS without an account, include the number of a guardian you added so we can find your records.
  3. Say whether you want to delete everything (your account) or only some data (for example, your SOS history and evidence, keeping your account).
  4. We confirm the request with you on your registered number, then complete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

You can remove or change guardians yourself at any time in the app’s Settings, without contacting us.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number and address
  • Your guardians’ names and numbers
  • Your SOS history: alert times, locations and addresses
  • Evidence video and audio files
  • Control room notes about your alerts

What is kept, and for how long

  • If an alert is part of an ongoing police investigation or a legal request, we keep only the records that case needs, for as long as the law requires, and delete them afterwards.
  • Copies in encrypted backups and server security logs are overwritten within BACKUP/LOG RETENTION, e.g. 30 days.
  • WhatsApp messages already delivered to your guardians stay on their phones; we cannot delete them.

Your other rights

  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing the same address.
  • Withdraw consent by uninstalling the app and asking us to delete your data. SOS features need location, camera and microphone access to work.

These rights are provided under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law.

6. Children

DEVI is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used DEVI, contact us and we will delete the data.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date above, and show the new terms in the app before they apply to you.
